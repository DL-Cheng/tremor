Hand Tremor Screening

This device measures hand tremor frequency & amplitude via a 3-axis accelerometer, to help characterize tremor patterns across different test positions.

Screening tool only — not a medical diagnosis. Results are generated by rule-based heuristics for research & educational demonstration. Always consult a qualified neurologist for clinical evaluation.

1. Choose Test Position

Different tremor types are most visible in different postures. Select the one your clinician or protocol specifies.

2. Get Ready

Rest Position

Sit comfortably. Rest your forearm on your lap or a table. Hold the device loosely in an open palm — do not grip it tightly, as active gripping engages forearm muscles and can mask a true resting tremor. Stay relaxed for the full duration.

  • Device is powered on and connected (see status indicator, top-right)
  • Stay as still and relaxed as the position allows
  • Recording lasts 15 seconds
